Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy describes how Obsidian Tasks Sync ("the app") handles your information.

Data the app accesses

With your permission, the app accesses your Google Tasks data (task lists and tasks, including titles, notes, due dates and completion status) through the Google Tasks API, using the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks. It does not access any other Google data.

How the data is used

Google Tasks data is used only to synchronize tasks between Google Tasks and your Obsidian vault. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

All data, including the OAuth access and refresh tokens, is stored locally on your own computer. The app has no backend server, and your data is never sent to the developer or to any third party. The only network communication is directly between your computer and Google's APIs.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete locally stored data, delete the app's token and configuration files from your computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/VeriVeryFY/obsidian-tasks-app/issues
