Obsidian Tasks Sync

Obsidian Tasks Sync is a personal tool that synchronizes tasks between an Obsidian vault and Google Tasks.

What it does

  • Reads your Google Task lists and tasks.
  • Creates, updates and completes tasks in Google Tasks to match the tasks in your Obsidian notes, and vice versa.
  • Runs locally on your own computer. There is no server and no account on our side.

Google permissions

The app requests access to the Google Tasks API only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks). It does not access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service.
